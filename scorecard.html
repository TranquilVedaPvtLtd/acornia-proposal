Section 03

NTA / ETA Business Split

Fresh business is measured separately for new and existing investors to ensure RMs are rewarded for both acquisition and deepening.

NTA

New to Acorn

Investors whose record was created during the evaluation period. Any lumpsum purchase or SIP registration from these investors counts as NTA fresh business.

  • Investor created within the period
  • First-time investment captured
  • Measures acquisition capability
ETA

Existing to Acorn

Investors whose record existed before the evaluation period began. Additional lumpsum or SIP investments from these investors count as ETA fresh business.

  • Investor existed before the period
  • Additional investments captured
  • Measures deepening capability
Annualized Value Formula
Annualized Value = Lumpsum + (Monthly SIP × 12)
Section 04

Scoring Algorithm

Each parameter is scored 0-100, then multiplied by its weight. The total produces a single composite rating for each RM.

Total Score
Total Score = Σ (Parameter Score × Weight)
where each Parameter Score is normalized to 0-100 range

Section 07

Expert Validation

Validated by 5 independent expert analyses. Below are the consensus recommendations that shaped the final scorecard design.

AUM Growth Threshold Revised

The "Outstanding" threshold for AUM growth was lowered from 8% to 5% monthly to reflect realistic market conditions and portfolio sizes.

5/5 unanimous

Merge Redundant Activity Parameters

Activity Logging (P3.3) and Zero-Activity Days (P4.2) overlap significantly. Recommendation to consolidate into a single composite activity metric.

4/5 agree

Rebalance Gold Coin Values for Business

Lumpsum coin values at 25L+ (25 coins) may be too generous relative to SIP registrations (5 coins). Suggested ratio adjustment to better incentivize recurring flows.

4/5 agree

Add Actual Learning Measures to P4

The Learning & Discipline perspective currently measures compliance hygiene, not learning. Consider adding training completion, certification tracking, or knowledge assessments.

4/5 agree

SIP Coin Vesting After 2 Installments

Gold coins for new SIPs should vest only after 2 successful installments to prevent gaming through quick registrations followed by cancellations.

3/5 agree

3-Month Non-Compensatory Pilot

Run the scorecard for 3 months in observation mode before linking it to compensation or appraisals. Allows threshold calibration with real data.
