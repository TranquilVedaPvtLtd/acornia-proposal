Section 3

NTA & ETA Definitions

Two critical business metrics that quantify new and incremental investor value.

NTA -- New to Acorn

Investors onboarded for the first time during the period. Measures the RM's ability to acquire entirely new relationships.

  • First-time lumpsum investments
  • First-time SIP registrations
  • Annualized value computation
  • Target: Rs 50L per RM per month

ETA -- Existing to Acorn

Additional investments from existing investors into new schemes or top-ups. Measures wallet-share deepening.

  • Top-up lumpsum investments
  • New SIPs from existing investors
  • Switch-in from external AMCs
  • Target: Rs 75L per RM per month
Annualized Value = Lumpsum + (Monthly SIP x 12)

Section 4

Scoring & Ratings

Each parameter is scored 0-100, then weighted. The composite score maps to a performance rating.

Final Score = SUM( Parameter Score x Weight )
Outstanding 90 -- 100
Excellent 80 -- 89
Good 70 -- 79
Developing 55 -- 69
Below Average < 55
All thresholds and weights are admin-configurable. Ratings can be adjusted per quarter based on business priorities without changing the underlying scorecard structure.

Section 7

Expert Validation

Key consensus findings from cross-functional review of the scorecard framework.

Financial weight of 40% is appropriate

Revenue-generating activities should remain the dominant perspective, reflecting core RM accountability.

Discipline perspective adds behavioral depth

Tracking daily habits like call targets and zero-activity days creates accountability beyond pure output metrics.

Gold coin system drives daily engagement

Gamification with immediate rewards is more motivating than monthly reviews alone. Streak bonuses are particularly effective.

NTA/ETA split clarifies acquisition vs. deepening

Separating new investor value from existing investor growth prevents masking of weak acquisition with top-ups.

CRM automation eliminates bias

Fully automated scoring from CRM data removes subjective manager assessments and ensures consistency across branches.

Quarterly weight recalibration is essential

Weights should shift with business priorities -- e.g., higher NTA weight during expansion phases, higher retention during market downturns.
